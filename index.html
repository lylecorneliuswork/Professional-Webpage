Personal investing

Jan 2025 - present

I manage my own portfolio around a core-satellite approach: passive index exposure through the Satrix S&P 500 ETF as the core, with individual equity positions, currently Pepkor, chosen through fundamental analysis as satellites.

Illustrative returns

The reasoning is straightforward: broad market exposure does the heavy lifting for long-term compounding, while single-stock picks are where I apply and test the valuation and macro views I'm building through coursework and my thesis work. It keeps the portfolio disciplined while still giving me a live testing ground for judgement, not just returns.

Honours thesis

"The impact of SARB monetary policy on South African ETFs: implications for portfolio allocation and investment strategy"

The thesis studies how changes in the SARB repo rate transmit into South African ETF returns, covering Satrix 40, NewGold, Satrix FINI, and Satrix CAPPED INDI, using a full quantitative pipeline in R: log returns, ADF stationarity testing, Granger causality, impulse response functions, and forecast error variance decomposition.

The pipeline also incorporates DCC-GARCH modelling to capture time-varying volatility and correlation between the repo rate and ETF returns, alongside wavelet analysis to examine how that relationship shifts across different time horizons and frequencies.
